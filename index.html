Process

How I work

Every project — mine or a client's — runs on the same Agile loop I've used as a certified Scrum Master, just compressed to fit the size of the job.

1

Discover

Understand the goal, the users, and the real constraints before writing a line of code.

2

Plan sprints

Break scope into short, demoable sprints — usually 1–2 weeks — with a clear definition of done.

3

Build & standup

Ship in small, tested increments. Daily standups keep blockers visible instead of buried.

4

Review & demo

Every sprint ends with a working demo, not a status report — the client sees real progress.

5

Retrospect

What worked, what didn't, what changes next sprint. Small corrections, made early and often.

6

Ship & iterate

Release, gather real feedback, and loop back into planning for the next cycle.

↻ repeats every cycle, start to launch

SLM Domiciliation

Company domiciliation & compliance SaaS, France

A full company-domiciliation platform for the French market, built as a Turborepo monorepo: public marketing site, subscription funnel, client portal and back-office admin panel, sharing one API and type-safe packages across all three front-ends. Handles KYC verification, e-signature, subscription billing, company-registry lookups (SIREN/SIRET and director matching), branded PDF contracts, attestations and invoices, and an exportable regulatory register of domiciled companies and their representatives.

Security was built in from the start rather than bolted on: rotating refresh tokens stored hashed, bcrypt at cost 12 everywhere including internal tooling, mandatory email verification before a user record even exists, TOTP for staff accounts, AES-256-GCM for every secret at rest, and HMAC-verified webhooks with an event ledger to block replay attacks. Separate rate-limit budgets protect auth, webhooks, public forms and general traffic independently.

Spartan Couriers

On-demand courier & delivery platform, UK

A courier booking platform with a customer site and app flow, driver onboarding and a back-office admin. Customers book in under a minute and follow the parcel live; drivers go through a compliance checklist (licence, DBS, hire and reward insurance, goods in transit) before they are approved; staff run the day from a live dashboard with fleet view, dispatch, driver approvals, payments, withdrawals and support tickets.

Restaurant Billing Platform

Offline-first billing SaaS for restaurants, India

A billing system for restaurants, cafes and quick-service shops that keeps working when the internet drops. The shop app caches its files and photos, journals every action offline and replays it in order once it reconnects; a small on-premises hub lets several devices in one shop share work with no connection at all. The same app is packaged as a web app, desktop apps (Tauri) and phone apps (Capacitor).

Front of house covers dine-in tables with per-guest ordering, kitchen tickets with live timelines, split payments (cash, UPI, card) with CGST/SGST invoices, and five printable bill styles for 58 mm to A4 printers. A built-in design studio produces printed menus, table cards, TV boards, WhatsApp posts and a QR menu, with prices staying linked to the live menu. Dishes carry Hindi and Gujarati names for staff and kitchen tickets. Owners get a daily profit dashboard (sales, GST collected, kitchen speed, top items, staff performance) and staff management with roles, shifts, attendance, leave and pay.

A separate master console runs the business side: customers with GSTIN, plans and per-shop pricing, renewals and invoices, partners, hosting, device approvals, support and system health. The demo runs on 301 sample shops. The audit log is hash-chained, and the interface is available in Hindi and Gujarati.
