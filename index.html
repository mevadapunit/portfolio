Process

How I work

Every project — mine or a client's — runs on the same Agile loop I've used as a certified Scrum Master, just compressed to fit the size of the job.

1

Discover

Understand the goal, the users, and the real constraints before writing a line of code.

2

Plan sprints

Break scope into short, demoable sprints — usually 1–2 weeks — with a clear definition of done.

3

Build & standup

Ship in small, tested increments. Daily standups keep blockers visible instead of buried.

4

Review & demo

Every sprint ends with a working demo, not a status report — the client sees real progress.

5

Retrospect

What worked, what didn't, what changes next sprint. Small corrections, made early and often.

6

Ship & iterate

Release, gather real feedback, and loop back into planning for the next cycle.

↻ repeats every cycle, start to launch

SLM Domiciliation

Company domiciliation & compliance SaaS, France

A full company-domiciliation platform for the French market, built as a Turborepo monorepo: public marketing site, subscription funnel, client portal and back-office admin panel, sharing one API and type-safe packages across all three front-ends. Handles KYC verification, e-signature, subscription billing and an exportable regulatory register of domiciled companies and their representatives.

Security was built in from the start rather than bolted on: rotating refresh tokens stored hashed, bcrypt at cost 12 everywhere including internal tooling, mandatory email verification before a user record even exists, TOTP for staff accounts, AES-256-GCM for every secret at rest, and HMAC-verified webhooks with an event ledger to block replay attacks. Separate rate-limit budgets protect auth, webhooks, public forms and general traffic independently.
